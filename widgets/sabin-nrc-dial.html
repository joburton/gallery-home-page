NRC — Noise Reduction Coefficient

A single number from 0 to 1 rating how much sound a material absorbs on average across four mid-range frequencies. 0 reflects almost everything; 1 absorbs almost everything. It is a simplification, but it is the number on every acoustic panel's spec sheet.

The Sabin

Named after Wallace Clement Sabine, who founded architectural acoustics. One sabin is the absorption of one square foot of perfectly absorptive surface. Multiply a material's NRC by its area for its total absorption.

SABINS = NRC × AREA (ft²) — this demo assumes 300 ft².
